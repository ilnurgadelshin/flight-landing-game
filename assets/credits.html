Flight deck & scenery credits

Built with free, openly licensed assets. Thank you to their creators.

Boeing 737-800 cockpit

Adapted from Boeing 737-800 Cockpit by hakai315, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Optimized geometry with the source shell proportions retained, baked cabin shading, runtime surface finishes, and working instruments and controls added.

Parked aircraft

B737 and A320 models by amvlab, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Scale and materials adapted; undercarriage added.

Landscape

Public-domain USDA NAIP aerial imagery, distributed by USGS / The National Map. Repositioned and adapted for fictional Westhaven scenery. Terrain uses public-domain USGS 3DEP elevation data, sampled and locally graded around the fictional airport.

Approach buildings and roads

Building footprints adapted from Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints, under CDLA Permissive 2.0. Roofs and most facades are procedural interpretations. Selected nearby walls use Modular Factory Facade and Modular Urban Apartments Facade by James Ray Cock / Poly Haven, under CC0. Wall bays, textures and glass are adapted and optimized. Airport gate halls are original project geometry. Five additional roof outlines were traced from the bundled public-domain NAIP imagery to fill prominent omissions. Selected road centerlines are public-domain U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb data. Reprojected and adapted around fictional Westhaven; widths and fixtures interpreted. This repackaging is not an official Census Bureau product.

Woodland

Canopy imagery baked from Tree Small 02 by Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven, under CC0. Also baked from Pine Sapling Small and Fir Sapling Medium, models by Rico Cilliers and photos by Rob Tuytel, also CC0. Also includes Jacaranda Tree by Rico Cilliers with guidance by Rob Tuytel, and Island Tree 02 scanned/processed by Rob Tuytel and cleaned/processed by Rico Cilliers, both CC0. Nine forms, four azimuths at three elevations each, plus simplified 3D geometry for nearby trees on high quality; scale, materials and coloration adapted for the fictional landscape.

Surface materials

Powered by Poly Haven: Aerial Grass Rock and Aerial Asphalt 01, plus Leather White and Poly Wool Herringbone adapted into cockpit liner and upholstery detail, under CC0.

Three.js, cannon-es and Meshoptimizer are used under their MIT licenses. These credits concern the game’s visual assets; the airport and navigation data are fictional.
