Flight deck & scenery credits

Built with free, openly licensed assets. Thank you to their creators.

Boeing 737-800 cockpit

Adapted from Boeing 737-800 Cockpit by hakai315, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Optimized geometry, adjusted shell and materials, baked shading, and working instruments and controls added.

Parked aircraft

B737 and A320 models by amvlab, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Scale and materials adapted; undercarriage added.

Landscape

Public-domain USDA NAIP aerial imagery, distributed by USGS / The National Map. Repositioned and adapted for fictional Westhaven scenery.

Woodland

Canopy imagery baked from Tree Small 02 by Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven, under CC0. Four views, scale and coloration adapted for the fictional landscape.

Surface materials

Powered by Poly Haven: Aerial Grass Rock and Aerial Asphalt 01, under CC0.

Three.js, cannon-es and Meshoptimizer are used under their MIT licenses. These credits concern the game’s visual assets; the airport and navigation data are fictional.
